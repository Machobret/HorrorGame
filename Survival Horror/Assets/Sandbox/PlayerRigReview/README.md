# Player rig review

Open `PlayerRig_Review.unity` and enter Play Mode. The scene spawns the current gameplay player through the template. Use the weapon buttons while standing or moving to try the actual aim and reload poses with loaded weapons and reserve ammo.

- **F6** freezes or resumes the pose. **F7** advances one gameplay frame from a frozen pose.
- **F8** cycles three-quarter, front, side, and rear views. Middle-drag orbits and the wheel zooms.
- **F9** toggles the character mesh and equipment. **F10** toggles the skeleton overlay.
- **Arms close-up** frames the upper body. The left arm is blue and the right arm is orange. An elbow angle of 180 degrees is a straight arm.
- **Elbow axes** displays each forearm bone's local X, Y, and Z axes in red, green, and blue.

The standalone sandbox uses the template's pause controller, normal movement and weapon bindings, and the same player mesh as the game.
