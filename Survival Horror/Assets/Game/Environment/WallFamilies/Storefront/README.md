# Storefront

Assign **Storefront.asset** to an exterior wall or to a single wall in House Properties. Its primary panel is 2 m wide. The 1 m and 0.5 m alternatives are available in Best Fit. The painted frame and gravel glass each have their own material slot.

**Segments** holds the panel and opening prefabs. **Meshes** holds their FBXs. The door surround fits the 0.9 × 2.1 m glass door and the window surround fits a 1 × 1 m insert. The door retains its native Horror Engine prefab and shared entry and exit behaviour.

The editable panel, door and window templates are in `ArtSource/Environment/Storefront/House Family Templates.blend`.
