## Generation prompt

Create a production-ready flat albedo texture sheet for the SIDES of four worn cardboard ammunition boxes in a PS1 / PSX survival horror game. Square 1024x1024 canvas divided into exactly FOUR equal horizontal strips, each 1024 wide by 256 high, filling every pixel with printed paper. No gaps, no borders between strips, no perspective, no 3D boxes, no lighting, no cast shadows. Each strip is a single long horizontal carton side panel, with subtle fiber grain, worn ink, scuffed corners, creases and small age stains. Graphic style: restrained vintage industrial packaging, chunky pixel-art shapes, muted limited palette, legible large condensed block lettering, suitable for reduction to 256x64 pixels per strip. From TOP to BOTTOM: 1) dark desaturated TEAL paper, off-white text '9mm' prominently and 'PISTOL AMMUNITION', a narrow aged ochre stripe near the top and tiny black barcode at right. 2) faded MUSTARD YELLOW paper with near-black text '12 GAUGE' prominently and 'SHOTGUN SHELLS', small red accent stripe, black barcode at right. 3) dark RUST-BROWN paper with aged cream text '.357 MAGNUM' prominently and '6 ROUNDS', thin muted red border near top and bottom, black barcode at right; match a weathered brown .357 ammo carton. 4) dark OLIVE GREEN paper with cream text '.300 WIN MAG' prominently and 'RIFLE CARTRIDGES', thin beige band near top, barcode at right. Keep labels centered within each panel, safely inset vertically and horizontally; no text crossing strip boundaries. No modern glossy finish, neon colors, photo-realistic detail, weapons or bullets pictured. Output only the usable texture atlas.
