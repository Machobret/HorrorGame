### Edit prompt

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. The attached four-option atlas is a design reference. Isolate and edit ONLY the single tobacco-brown and dark olive fabric satchel inventory frame in the BOTTOM RIGHT of the atlas. Output one clean standalone inventory background at high resolution with genuinely transparent edges, preserve its upright approximately 0.84 width-to-height (about 550×660) rectangular footprint and straight-on flat orthographic view. Remove the entire folded-back top bag flap / overhanging lip. Specifically replace that wide flap with a narrow strip of worn tobacco brown leather identical to the left/right/bottom rim in width and treatment. The leather welt plus the understated dark zipper seam should continue cleanly across the top and turn around the upper corners exactly as they do around the lower corners. This gives a simple low-profile border of equal thickness on all four sides. Retain the recognizable subtly scratched dark tobacco leather, quiet stitching or zipper detail, modest rounded corners, and the dark muted olive charcoal canvas lining of that bottom-right original. The visible canvas should now extend all the way up to the new narrow top rim and look like one large uninterrupted matte plane. Keep subtle believable canvas grain but reduce fabric folds and dramatic real-world depth, a flatter and more restrained game UI appearance. Careful tonal cohesion rather than gloss or bright trim. Narrow rim around 4 percent of the total width on each side. Equal testable top and bottom rim silhouettes. No flap, handle, raised lid, protruding zipper pull, hardware, straps, divided pockets, inventory squares, items, characters, text, logo, environmental light, floor, or full UI. Preserve outside transparency, whole silhouette visible, clean margins. Produce only the single new panel, never the atlas.
```

Use case: product-mockup.

Equal footprint and scale, each about 4:5 width to height, centered in its own quadrant, with generous clear gutters and outer margins so each can be cut out independently. Entire silhouettes visible. Straight-on, orthographic front view, edges stay horizontal and vertical, consistently aligned and broadly rectangular. Each option has a large uninterrupted dark matte low-contrast interior, with about 85% of its width clear for the real item grid and lower tablet area that will be composited later. No internal separators or fabricated UI slots. Thin perimeter relative to the opening. Photoreal cutout product photography, soft even studio light, legible leather grain, creases, a little rubbed finish and worn edges, believable and restrained use. Bottom left: charcoal-brown industrial leather equipment tray with a shallow dark suede insert, thin welt, subtly aged blackened-metal corner reinforcement and a few small recessed rivets. Hardware confined to the border.
