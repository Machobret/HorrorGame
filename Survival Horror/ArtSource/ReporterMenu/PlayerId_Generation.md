```text
Use case: product-mockup.
Asset type: one review atlas of four blank player ID panel backgrounds for a survival-horror game about an adventurous field reporter.
Art direction: paired with an open, worn tobacco-brown leather reporter satchel with a muted olive-charcoal canvas lining, rubbed leather grain, stitched rim, and restrained aged hardware. Carry those material tones over in small touches so these read as credentials belonging to the same person. Real photographed physical materials, subtle handling wear, matte surfaces, soft neutral studio lighting.
Composition: exactly four isolated horizontal reporter credentials in a perfectly clear 2 × 2 contact sheet, equal visual scale, each approximately 1.9 times as wide as tall. Consistent orthographic face-on view without perspective skew. Four complete silhouettes, generous transparent gutters and outer clearance all the way around. Wide atlas canvas. Authentic alpha transparency around each card without cast shadows outside the edges. Maximize useful pixels and give each option an uninterrupted, flat, low-contrast face covering at least 88 percent of its width. Keep all embellishment at the extreme perimeter.
Top left: warm buff paper press credential in a thin, stitched tobacco-brown leather slip case, soft worn corners, understated and practical.
Top right: muted slate-brown matte phenolic credential with a smoked brass edge and restrained flush corner reinforcements, a light industrial influence. Keep the face moderately dark and completely even.
Bottom left: pale warm-gray fibrous cardstock inset in very thin olive-charcoal woven canvas binding, subtle edge stitching, traces of tobacco leather at the two short ends.
Bottom right: weathered chestnut leather ID wallet folded flat under one single broad muted tan matte card insert, rolled leather edges and slightly rounded worn corners. No fold, seam, pocket or hardware interrupts the insert.
Every card face is entirely empty, including the top, left, and lower right. The developer will separately place the character name and branding, a portrait of undecided proportions on the left, equipment icons on the upper right, and a one- or two-line health stamp on the lower right. Do not draw any placeholders, portrait outlines, photo window, boxes, divider lines, type, labels, numbers, logos, stamps, badges, holograms, strings, people, items, or scene elements. This is a photo-real blank UI background atlas.
```
