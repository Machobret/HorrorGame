What the decisions mean

Keep: visually suitable placement candidate. Repair: missing art, fallback material or geometry cleanup. Review: scale, assembly or content-scope decision. Background only: photo-textured proxy, unsuitable for close inspection. Exclude: detached source samples/layouts that need splitting or rebuilding. No model is deleted by this catalog.
