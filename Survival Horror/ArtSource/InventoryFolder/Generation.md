# Inventory paper art

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, text-to-image mode, transparent background enabled, one asset per call. No external reference image was supplied. Imported as single sprites, maximum 1024, alpha transparency enabled, no mipmaps, uncompressed. These are presentation textures, not inventory logic.

## Manila folder

Output: `Assets/Game/UI/InventoryFolder/Textures/Inventory_ManilaFolder.png`

Prompt:

Game UI texture asset, not a screenshot or mockup. One empty aged yellow manila document folder viewed exactly straight down, fully open and flattened, landscape 4:3 silhouette. A modest index tab protrudes from the top left. Warm ochre paper, softly worn corners, subtle creases, restrained scuffs, darker folded edges and a slight central fold. Most of its interior is a broad nearly blank light mustard surface, usable behind item icons. PSX survival horror inventory aesthetic with believable photographed paper texture, restrained detail, no heavy pixelation. Transparent background around the entire isolated folder, no cast shadow beyond the silhouette. No text, no lettering, no objects, no clips, no pockets obscuring the interior. Fill most of the image while keeping the whole folder and tab inside its bounds.

## Torn notebook

Output: `Assets/Game/UI/InventoryFolder/Textures/Inventory_TornNotebook.png`

Prompt:

Game UI texture asset, not a screenshot or mockup. One isolated vertically oriented torn notebook sheet viewed exactly straight down and flat. Ivory paper with slight yellowing, a ragged torn spiral binding edge along the left, small punched holes and a few paper fibres. Faint horizontal gray-blue ruled lines and a subtle faded red margin line. Centre is clean and pale for readable item descriptions. A few natural folds and softly worn lower corners. Grounded PSX survival horror prop aesthetic, photographed paper texture, restrained detail, no heavy pixelation. Transparent background around the sheet and through the punched holes. No handwriting, no printed text, no illustrations, no objects, no notebook behind it. Whole sheet within image bounds.

## Font

Courier Prime Regular and its OFL license were downloaded from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/courierprime and are retained in the feature's Fonts folder. Control labels reference the existing project's 3270 tablet font.
